01

Hide the subject

The tweet is about Joe's Diner. That name is swapped for a mask token, so the model has to work it out from everything else.

02

Read the words

A Twitter-trained language model reads what's left. "Absolutely delicious" still carries the mood, even with the subject gone.

03

Read the photo

A Vision Transformer cuts the image into 14 × 14 = 196 patches and reads each one.

04

Give the answer

Words and photo are combined to answer two questions: how does the author feel about the hidden subject, and what kind of thing is it?
