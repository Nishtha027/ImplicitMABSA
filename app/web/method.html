Stage 1 of 4

Hide the aspect

The tweet names what it is about: Joe's Diner. If that aspect appears in the text, it is swapped for the encoder's mask token, so the model has to work it out from everything else.

This is how we simulate an implicit aspect
Stage 2 of 4

Read the words

A Twitter RoBERTa encoder, fine-tuned end to end, turns every remaining token into a state. "Absolutely delicious" still carries the mood, even with the subject gone.

Up to 96 tokens per tweet
Stage 3 of 4

Read the photo

The image is resized to 224 × 224 and cut into 14 × 14 = 196 patches. A frozen Vision Transformer encodes them once, and the features are stored for training.

197 tokens: 196 patches plus a class token
Stage 4 of 4

Fuse and predict

The text and image features are combined, and two heads answer two questions: how does the author feel about the hidden aspect, and what kind of thing is it?
